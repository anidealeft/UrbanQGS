Large-Scale Urban Surface Reconstruction via Analytic Curvature Consistency in Quadratic Gaussian Splatting

A curvature-driven framework for reconstructing complete, detailed, and compact urban surfaces from UAV imagery.

Abstract

Reconstructing urban geometry at city scale

Large-scale 3D surface reconstruction at the city scale from UAV imagery is a core task for building digital-twin cities. Although 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) and its variants have achieved remarkable progress in radiance-field reconstruction, existing methods still struggle with scale-varying structural complexity and diverse urban textures: geometry is often incomplete in low-frequency, weak-texture regions over large areas, while in high-frequency regions with intricate details, disordered primitive distributions tend to cause floating artifacts and outliers.

To address these issues, we propose UrbanQGS, a large-scale urban reconstruction framework built on quadratic Gaussian splatting. We inherit quadratic surface Gaussians as scene primitives and use their analytic curvature as the central driving signal to perform a two-stage geometric refinement: (1) A self-supervised geometric constraint based on cross-view curvature consistency, which effectively fills geometric holes in weak-texture planar regions. (2) A curvature-aware adaptive representation allocation strategy that reweights, recalibrates, and ranks densification gradients, guiding split and clone operations toward geometrically complex regions while standard pruning removes redundant primitives.

In addition, we integrate a monocular normal prior to correct global orientation drift during large-scale reconstruction. We further assess the compatibility of UrbanQGS with partitioned large-scene reconstruction using a visibility-aware block-wise protocol with hardware-constrained per-block budgets. The blocks are trained sequentially on a single GPU. Experiments on large-scale UAV datasets demonstrate that, compared with state-of-the-art 3DGS-based large-scale reconstruction methods, UrbanQGS maintains superior geometric quality while substantially reducing redundant Gaussians and mesh complexity, yielding finer and more complete geometric structures, while qualitative results on our custom-collected dataset indicate its compatibility with a partitioned large-scene reconstruction pipeline.

Curvature as a geometric signal

Qualitative results

Finer structures with fewer redundant primitives

UrbanQGS maintains strong geometric quality across large urban scenes while reducing floating artifacts and unnecessary representation growth.
